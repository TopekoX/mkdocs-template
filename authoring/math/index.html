    Mathematics¶

    Math uses MathJax through pymdownx.arithmatex. Use $...$ or \(...\) for inline math and $$...$$ or \[...\] for display math.

    Aligned equations¶

    \[ \begin{aligned} \mathcal{L}(\theta) &= -\frac{1}{N}\sum_{i=1}^{N}\left[y_i\log \hat{y}_i + (1-y_i)\log(1-\hat{y}_i)\right] \\ \frac{\partial \mathcal{L}}{\partial \theta_j} &= \frac{1}{N}\sum_{i=1}^{N}(\hat{y}_i - y_i)\,x_{ij} \\ \theta_j &\leftarrow \theta_j - \eta\,\frac{\partial \mathcal{L}}{\partial \theta_j} \end{aligned} \]

    Softmax:

    \[ \operatorname{softmax}(z)_k = \frac{e^{z_k}}{\sum_{j=1}^{K} e^{z_j}} \]

    Inside other components¶

    Bayes' rule

    \[ P(A \mid B) = \frac{P(B \mid A)\,P(A)}{P(B)} \]

    Inline works too: the posterior \(P(A \mid B)\) is proportional to the likelihood.

    \(\operatorname{Var}(X) = \mathbb{E}[X^2] - (\mathbb{E}[X])^2\)

    \(\nabla f = \left(\frac{\partial f}{\partial x_1}, \dots, \frac{\partial f}{\partial x_n}\right)\)

    Limitations¶

    • MathJax loads from a CDN, so math needs network access.
    • A literal dollar sign must be written \$.
    • Prefer one equation block per $$ pair; keep blank lines out of display math.
    • Each \\ line break needs an environment such as aligned or cases.
